# Portugal Safe Project Context

## Overview

Portugal Safe is an independent public-safety application designed for people living in, travelling through, or visiting Portugal.

The goal is to make reliable safety information easier to access by combining structured information from official and public sources.

## Safety Coverage

Portugal Safe focuses on:

- wildfires and natural fires;
- extreme heat and drought;
- storms and severe weather risks;
- emergency notifications;
- first-aid information;
- emergency preparation;
- local safety information.

## International and Cross-Border Approach

Portugal Safe is designed for an international environment.

Current focus:

- Portugal: broad public-safety coverage;
- Spain and France: wildfire-related information.

The long-term vision is expansion toward a wider European safety information network.

## Multilingual Support

The application is designed for:

- Portuguese residents;
- expatriates;
- tourists;
- international travellers.

The platform supports five languages to improve access to safety information.

## AI Safety Assistant

Future development includes YARA (Your Always Ready Assistant), an AI-supported safety assistant designed to help users access structured safety knowledge.

Potential capabilities include:

- emergency preparation guidance;
- first-aid information support;
- safety questions;
- structured emergency knowledge assistance.

AI support remains limited by safety boundaries and does not replace emergency services.

## Official Data and Safety Workflows

Portugal Safe integrates with structured safety information workflows using official and public sources.

Examples include:

- emergency information;
- wildfire-related data;
- weather and risk information;
- local safety information.

## Emergency Validator Role

Portugal Safe Emergency Validator is an open-source technical component within this broader safety ecosystem.

The validator improves data reliability by checking:

- required fields;
- source information;
- timestamps;
- location context;
- categories;
- severity levels;
- language metadata;
- safety instructions.

The validator helps prevent unreliable emergency data from entering applications.

## Safety Boundary

Portugal Safe does not replace official authorities.

In emergencies, users must contact official emergency services.

Call 112 for immediate emergencies.
